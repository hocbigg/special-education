Great Courses

This directory features standout open courses, comprehensive university lectures, and audiovisual deep-dives that extend beyond core introductory coursework to build deep intuition in behavioral sciences, neurodevelopmental differences, accessibility standards, and inclusive education policy.

Neurodevelopmental & Learning Profiles

  • Overcoming Dyslexia (Coursera / Yale University / Dr. Sally Shaywitz & Dr. Bennett Shaywitz)
  • ADHD: Everyday Strategies for Elementary Students (Coursera / State University of New York at Buffalo / Dr. Greg Fabiano)
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (Coursera / University of California, Santa Cruz / Dr. Ninet Moradi)
  • Supporting Children with Difficulties in Reading and Writing (Coursera / University of London & UCL Institute of Education / Dr. Jenny Thomson)
  • Teaching Children with Visual Impairment: Creating Empowering Classrooms (Coursera / University of Cape Town / Dr. Brian Watermeyer)
  • Severe to Profound Intellectual Disability: Circles of Care and Education (Coursera / University of Cape Town / Dr. Judith McKenzie)
  • Understanding ADHD (OpenLearn / The Open University)
  • Understanding Autism (OpenLearn / The Open University)

Applied Behavior Analysis & Behavioral Supports

  • Everyday Parenting: The ABCs of Child Rearing (Coursera / Yale University / Dr. Alan E. Kazdin)
  • Managing ADHD, Autism, Learning Disabilities, and Concussion in School (Coursera / University of Colorado System)
  • Teaching Character and Creating Positive Classrooms (Coursera / Relay Graduate School of Education / Dave Levin)

Digital Accessibility & Universal Design

  • W3Cx: Introduction to Web Accessibility (edX / World Wide Web Consortium)
  • GTx: Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Accessibility (edX / Georgia Institute of Technology)
  • Disability Awareness and Support (Coursera / University of Pittsburgh / Dr. Mary Goldberg)
  • An Introduction to Accessibility and Inclusive Design (Coursera / University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign)

Inclusive Systems, Policy & Advocacy

  • Inclusive Education: Essential Knowledge for Success (FutureLearn / Queensland University of Technology / Prof. Linda Graham)
  • ColumbiaX: Inclusive Teaching: Supporting All Students in the College Classroom (edX / Columbia University)
  • Disability Inclusion in Education: Building Systems of Support (Coursera / University of Cape Town / Dr. Judith McKenzie)
