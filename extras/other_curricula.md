# Other Curricula

These alternative curricula, professional standards, open-access modular roadmaps, and accredited institutional benchmarks provide different pedagogical frameworks and self-directed study pathways across special education, applied behavior analysis, and inclusive instructional design.

- [Initial Practice-Based Professional Preparation Standards for Special Educators (Council for Exceptional Children)](https://exceptionalchildren.org/standards/initial-preparation-standards)
- [Special Education Course Enhancement Modules & Open Curricular Matrix (The IRIS Center / Vanderbilt University Peabody College)](https://iris.peabody.vanderbilt.edu)
- [Intensive Intervention Course Series & Practice Matrix (National Center on Intensive Intervention)](https://intensiveintervention.org)
- [Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Guidelines & Curricular Framework (CAST)](https://udlguidelines.cast.org)
- [BACB Course Sequence & Task List for Behavior Analysis in Education (Behavior Analyst Certification Board)](https://www.bacb.com)
- [BA (Honours) Education Studies (Primary) Special Educational Needs Pathway (The Open University)](https://www.open.ac.uk/courses/education/degrees/ba-education-studies-primary-q94)
