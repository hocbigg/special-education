# Other Curricula

These alternative curricula, professional standards, open-access modular roadmaps, and independent practitioner pathways offer diverse perspectives and structural blueprints for designing a self-directed course of study in special education.

- [Initial Practice-Based Professional Preparation Standards for Special Educators (Council for Exceptional Children)](https://exceptionalchildren.org/standards/initial-preparation-standards)
- [Course Enhancement Modules for Teacher Preparation (CEEDAR Center / University of Florida)](https://ceedar.education.ufl.edu/cems/)
- [Intensive Intervention Course Content & Training Series (National Center on Intensive Intervention)](https://intensiveintervention.org/training/course-content)
- [Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Guidelines & Curricular Framework (CAST)](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/)
- [Test Content Outlines & Task Lists for Behavior Analysts (Behavior Analyst Certification Board)](https://www.bacb.com/examination-information/)
- [Special Education Law & Advocacy Training Curriculum (Wrightslaw / Peter W. D. Wright & Pamela Darr Wright)](https://www.wrightslaw.com/advoc/articles/)
- [BA (Honours) Education Studies (Primary) Special Educational Needs Pathway (The Open University)](https://www.open.ac.uk/courses/education/degrees/ba-education-studies-primary-q94)
