Other Curricula

These alternative curricula, professional standards, open-access modular roadmaps, and independent practitioner pathways offer diverse perspectives and structural blueprints for designing a self-directed course of study in special education.

  • Initial Practice-Based Professional Preparation Standards for Special Educators (Council for Exceptional Children)
  • Course Enhancement Modules for Teacher Preparation (CEEDAR Center / University of Florida)
  • Intensive Intervention Course Content & Training Series (National Center on Intensive Intervention)
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Guidelines & Curricular Framework (CAST)
  • Test Content Outlines & Task Lists for Behavior Analysts (Behavior Analyst Certification Board)
  • Special Education Law & Advocacy Training Curriculum (Wrightslaw / Peter W. D. Wright & Pamela Darr Wright)
  • BA (Honours) Education Studies (Primary) Special Educational Needs Pathway (The Open University)
