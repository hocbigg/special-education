Other curricula

This curriculum is one of a few efforts to inform learners how they can pursue [Field] independently. Below are a few other such efforts.

  • Education for All: Disability, Diversity and Inclusion — a (currently occasionally offered) course on inclusive education practices and diversity in learning. (FutureLearn)
  • Accessible Online Learning System (Disability & Inclusion modules) — a free, self-paced set of courses on disability awareness, inclusion, and accommodations (publicly accessible). (NCDA Courses)
