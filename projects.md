# Projects

This page is a curated collection of step-by-step guides, open templates, and methodological walkthroughs for learning Special Education through hands-on, project-based practice.

> _What I cannot create, I do not understand — Richard Feynman._

These projects support learning throughout the curriculum. By constructing real-world pedagogical, diagnostic, behavioral, and assistive artifacts, learners develop deep practical competency alongside theoretical study.
