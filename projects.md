# Projects

This directory provides curated step-by-step guides, published methodological walkthroughs, and open templates for learning Special Education through hands-on practice. By creating pedagogical, diagnostic, behavioral, and assistive artifacts, self-directed learners bridge foundational theory and real-world application.

> _What I cannot create, I do not understand._ — Richard Feynman
